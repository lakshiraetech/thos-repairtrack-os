Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Everything you need to know about getting your workshop live on THOS RepairTrack OS.

How does the WhatsApp 1-Click estimate approval work?
When a technician submits a diagnostic estimate, THOS compiles the line items, parts, and labor into a compliant Meta WhatsApp Cloud API template. The customer receives a message with itemized pricing and two native interactive buttons: "✅ 1-Click Approve" and "❌ Reject / Request Call". Tapping "Approve" instantly records an audit log with timestamp and moves the ticket to WORK_IN_PROGRESS.
What is CollectIQ™ and how does it recover uncollected revenue?
CollectIQ™ is an automated escalation engine that triggers as soon as a vehicle reaches the REPAIRED status. It manages a strict escalation schedule: T+0 (Friendly invoice & express checkout), T+24h (Courtesy reminder), T+48h (Demurrage bay storage fee grace period warning), and T+72h (Formal commercial lien notice). When the customer pays, it automatically cancels pending reminders and issues the 6-digit gate OTP.
Can I use THOS RepairTrack OS on iPads, tablets, and phones?
Yes. THOS is completely web-native and responsive. Service advisors can perform walk-around intakes on an iPad or Android tablet right at the vehicle bay, while technicians can log OBD-II diagnostic fault codes and parts directly from their mobile phones.
How does the 6-digit Delivery OTP gate clearance work?
When an intake ticket is registered, a cryptographic 6-digit OTP is generated and stored in hashed form. Once the customer clears the invoice balance, the OTP is revealed to them via WhatsApp. At vehicle release, the cashier enters the OTP; only when it matches does the system release the vehicle to DELIVERED status and dispatch the review booster.
Can we migrate from our existing workshop software?
Yes! Our onboarding team provides pre-built CSV import scripts for your customer lists, vehicle databases, and inventory parts catalogs. You can be up and running within less than 24 hours.
